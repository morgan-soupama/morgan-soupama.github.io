Rehabilitation of a fire hose reel network

Fluid Engineering Intern, HVAC & Hydraulic Design · Egis Bâtiment Antilles-Guyane

Alongside HVAC and hydraulic sizing for commercial and healthcare buildings, I worked on the design-build rehabilitation of the fire hose reel network of a penitentiary centre in Martinique. The existing network did not meet NF EN 671-1 and NF S62-201: 28 hose reels had insufficient pressure or non-compliant DN 40 piping.

  • Surveyed and located the 44 hose reels on site plans and identified the hydraulically worst-placed one (3rd floor of the cross-shaped building).
  • Sized a buried ring main of about 1.1 km, including 410 m of new pipe, with velocity kept under 3 m/s (APSAD R5).
  • Applied the generalised Bernoulli equation with major and minor head losses to set the required network pressure.
  • Sized the booster pump and the fire water tank, and contributed to the technical specification (CCTP, lot 22).

NASCAR drafting with a translating overset mesh

CFD of the slipstream effect during an overtake

Can STAR-CCM+ capture drafting when one car moves relative to another? We modelled two simplified Gen-6 stock cars (5 × 1.95 × 1.42 m) at about 320 km/h and moved the follower through an overset region to track its drag and lift coefficients as the gap closes.

  • Built static and dynamic cases in 2D and 3D, with the overset zone translating inside a refined control volume.
  • Realizable k-ε two-layer model with all-y+ wall treatment and 8 prism layers.
  • Compared static vs dynamic and 2D vs 3D drag and lift against the 0–10 m gap range; values matched published NASCAR coefficients (Cd ≈ 0.45–0.5).

Multiphase oil-jet cooling for e-motors

Oil-air CFD for a direct oil-cooling system

Novares develops direct oil cooling for electric motors. We simulated an oil jet impinging on a target surface, where the flow breaks into films and droplets, and then made the model affordable to run.
